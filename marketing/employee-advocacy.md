# Employee Advocacy Distribution Assistant

**Function:** Social amplification and distributed content operations  
**Source basis:** Advocacy Post Generator tool card  
**Documented design:** Four controlled, employee-ready versions of a campaign asset

## Problem

When an organization launches content, employees can extend its reach, but manually adapting source material for many individual contributors creates inconsistency and can introduce unsupported claims.

## How it works

```mermaid
flowchart LR
 A["Approved asset URL or document"] --> B["Extract verifiable promotional insights"]
 B --> C["Apply examples and channel style rules"]
 C --> D["Four employee-ready post formats"]
 D --> E["Employee review and publication"]
```

The input is an approved blog, report, webinar, case study, announcement or similar asset. The tool draws facts from the asset and follows a house style guide to produce four distinct formats:

1. **Conversational invitation:** Warm introduction to the resource.
2. **Question-led post:** Frames a relevant problem or discussion.
3. **Data snapshot:** Uses a metric only when it appears in the source.
4. **News-style announcement:** Concise summary of the asset's news value.

The tool card specifies limits on hashtags/emojis and format-specific structure. It does not invent metrics.

## Value to the organization

**Make earned content more reusable across employee channels** by providing consistent, evidence-checked starting points without asking each person to rewrite the resource from scratch. The employee still owns the judgment to edit and post.

**Scope:** Structured draft preparation, not automatic social publication, employee engagement tracking or measured advocacy reach.

**Suggested evaluation:** Asset-to-post turnaround time, adoption by participating employees and measured referral traffic or engagement.
