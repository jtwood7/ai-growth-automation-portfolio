# Sales AI Enablement

Tools that turn account research, customer evidence and commercial context into **better-prepared outreach, stronger sales conversations and more consistent proposal work**.

## Prospecting and outreach

| System | Core value |
|---|---|
| [Account Strategy Copilot](../enablement/account-strategy-copilot.md) | Public company intelligence, buying committee hypotheses, persona-specific strategy and tailored outreach |
| [Signal-Led BDR Research](../enablement/signal-led-bdr-research.md) | Detect attributable account signals and connect them to relevant buyer problems and approved proof |
| [Role-Tailored Outreach](role-tailored-outreach.md) | Consistent personalization across a contact list while preserving campaign structure |

## Sales evidence and deal support

| System | Core value |
|---|---|
| [Evidence-Grounded Messaging](../enablement/evidence-grounded-messaging.md) | Retrieve the right case study and translate verified outcomes into role-specific talking points |
| [RFP Response Assistant](../enablement/source-grounded-rfp-responses.md) | Extract questions and use approved sources to build structured, reviewable answers |
| [Vendor Comparison](competitive-rfp-analysis.md) | Compare proposal evidence by facet to inform competitive positioning |

For cross-system automation, see the [Rep Empowerment Engine](../docs/rep-empowerment.md).
