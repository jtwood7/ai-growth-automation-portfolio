The original GPT instructions, knowledge files and enterprise access are **not** part of this repository. The case studies document intended capabilities; they are not executable software.

### Synthetic thought experiment: source-backed outreach

**Fictional input**  
- Account: Example Distribution Group  
- Persona: VP of Operations  
- Observed event: A *fictional* press announcement dated September 15, 2026 says the company plans to expand its distribution footprint.  
- Approved evidence pack: *No approved proof supplied.*

**Responsible assistant behavior**  
1. Produce a neutral account brief that labels the source as hypothetical until verified.
2. Identify expansion planning as a **possible** operations concern, not a verified pain.
3. Ask for approved product context and case-study proof before making a product claim.
4. Draft a short outreach hypothesis without invented ROI, customer names or capabilities.
5. Provide a source checklist and explicitly flag any missing proof.

**Illustrative draft snippet (synthetic, not an actual GPT result):**  
> Hi [First name], I saw the announcement about your team's planned expansion. When operators add facilities, keeping processes consistent across sites can become a timely discussion. Would comparing notes on your rollout priorities be useful?

The example demonstrates expected **input/output discipline**, not a live system or claim about any real company.
